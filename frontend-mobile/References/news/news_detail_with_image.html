GST Update: New policy released for Gold & Diamond imports

Published 24 Oct 2024 • 09:30 AM
Reading Time 6 Min Read
Trade Law Compliance

The Ministry of Finance has officially released the revised Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework specifically targeting the import of precious metals and unmounted gemstones. This landmark decision aims to streamline the cross-border trade of high-value commodities while ensuring greater transparency in the supply chain.

Effective from the next fiscal quarter, the new directives replace the provisional measures introduced earlier this year. Under the new guidelines, authorized economic operators (AEOs) will benefit from a fast-track clearance process at major international ports, provided they adhere to the updated digital documentation standards.

Key Implications for Diamond Merchants

The policy introduces a nuanced classification for lab-grown diamonds, distinguishing them from natural stones for duty valuation. Industry analysts suggest this move will provide much-needed clarity for local manufacturers who have been navigating a grey area in customs declarations for the past eighteen months.
